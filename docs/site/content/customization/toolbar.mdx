Use `className` for styling. Use `icon` for an icon element, `asChild` for your element, and `hidden` to remove a default part. `hidden` on a named part, such as `Toolbar.Bold`, is shorthand for `<Toolbar.Slot slotId="text.bold" hidden />`.

Set `preset={false}` to choose the complete arrangement. Use `useEditorCommand(slotId)` for application controls. It supplies enabled state and refusal reasons.

Keep the caret when a toolbar button receives a mouse press. Call `preventDefault()` for buttons, but skip inputs, selects, and text areas. Hiding a button does not remove its API operation.

## Group your own controls

Put your controls in a `Toolbar.Group`. A group collapses into the **More** panel as a whole when the toolbar runs out of width, and its label becomes the heading of its panel section. Give a host group a `label`, or a `labelKey` from your translation catalog. The `id` of a built-in group, such as `review` or `zoom`, changes that group instead. TypeScript suggests the built-in ids through the `ChromeGroupId` type.

<FrameworkTabs>
<Framework value="react">

```tsx
<DocxEditor.Toolbar>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group id="contract" label="Contract" priority={15}>
    <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Action label="Ask legal" onSelect={askLegal} />
  </DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group id="review">
    <DocxEditor.Toolbar.AddComment />
  </DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group id="zoom" hidden />
</DocxEditor.Toolbar>
```

</Framework>
<Framework value="vue">

```vue
<DocxEditor.Toolbar>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group id="contract" label="Contract" :priority="15">
    <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Action label="Ask legal" @select="askLegal" />
  </DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group id="review">
    <DocxEditor.Toolbar.AddComment />
  </DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Group id="zoom" hidden />
</DocxEditor.Toolbar>
```

</Framework>
</FrameworkTabs>

A lower `priority` leaves the bar first. `hidden` on a group removes the whole group and its separator.

## Replace one control

`Toolbar.Slot` replaces one built-in control with your own content. Name the control with `slotId`. Your content keeps the control's place and collapses with its group.

<FrameworkTabs>
<Framework value="react">

```tsx
<DocxEditor.Toolbar>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Slot slotId="zoom.level">
    <MyZoomPicker />
  </DocxEditor.Toolbar.Slot>
</DocxEditor.Toolbar>
```

</Framework>
<Framework value="vue">

```vue
<DocxEditor.Toolbar>
  <DocxEditor.Toolbar.Slot slot-id="zoom.level">
    <MyZoomPicker />
  </DocxEditor.Toolbar.Slot>
</DocxEditor.Toolbar>
```

</Framework>
</FrameworkTabs>
