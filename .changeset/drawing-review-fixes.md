Charts now format elapsed times, fractions of a second, and pie and legend category labels, and place monthly and yearly date axes at even steps. Malformed TIFF, EMF, WMF, and WordArt content can no longer stall or exhaust memory, and a filled shape with a double outline paints its fill once. A drawing record's `placeholderGraphicKind` is now typed as `DrawingPlaceholderKind`.
