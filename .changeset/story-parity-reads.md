Formatting, list and table controls now act on the story the caret is in. In a header, footer, footnote or endnote you can insert a table, turn a numbered or bulleted list off, and see the list buttons reflect what the paragraph is. Increase Indent demotes a list item there as it does in the body, a numbered paragraph paints its marker, and selecting two paragraphs no longer empties the font size box.
