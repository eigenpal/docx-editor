A keep-with-next paragraph that does not fit in the space left on a page now splits there when its keep-lines and widow-control settings allow it, instead of moving whole to the next page. In documents that use Word 2013 or later layout, a paragraph in a keep-with-next chain that fits but leaves no room for the next paragraph also splits, and its last lines move to the next page.
