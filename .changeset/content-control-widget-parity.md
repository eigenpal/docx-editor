Content-control pop-ups offer shared keyboard navigation, regional date entry, month/year selection, viewport-aware placement, composable React/Vue parts, and host sessions for checkbox, picture, and building block gallery presses, where a picture control replaces its image, a gallery control inserts a block from the document's glossary, a control saved with empty content opens showing its glossary placeholder, a control emptied by deletion shows its prompt again, and control buttons appear on hover and at the caret. Legacy FORMCHECKBOX fields have matching box and caret geometry, larger pointer targets, and click/Space toggling, and a paragraph whose only content is a `w:sym` glyph now paints it.
