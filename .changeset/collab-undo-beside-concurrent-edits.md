In a collaboration session, a table row, column, table, or paragraph that two participants delete at the same time stays deleted when one of them undoes, and typing that a participant undoes no longer shows again in a copy that a concurrent join made. An undo next to moved blocks no longer removes another participant's text or leaves a moved table's empty paragraph without the identity that remote carets need. Fixes #1601.
