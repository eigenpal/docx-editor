Clicks now land where they are aimed. Clicking left of a line puts the caret at its start, right of it at its end, and clicking in a margin, an indent, the gap between lines or a table cell's padding resolves to the nearest text instead of wherever the browser guessed. Dragging across table cells selects the rectangle of cells rather than the text running through them.
