You can insert column, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut, and scatter charts, and edit a chart's type, elements, colors, and data in a Chart pane in React and Vue, or in your own pane with the `useSelectedChart` hook. You can also resize, wrap, copy, and paste a chart, and a floating picture or chart that you drag to another paragraph anchors there, so text wraps around it.
