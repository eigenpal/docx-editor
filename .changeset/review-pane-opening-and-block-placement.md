Add a `paneOpening` option to `reviewModule` so a host can keep the review pane closed until it opens it, an optional placement to `scrollToBlock`, and `getReviewItemsAt` and `getReviewItemRects` to find the review item under a pointer and the rectangles it paints. Escape now closes toolbar popups that were opened by a click, a reply in a review card shows its own author's color, and inputs you add to a review card keep focus.
