Add a `paneOpening` option to `reviewModule` so a host can keep the review pane closed until it opens it, and an optional placement to `scrollToBlock`. Escape now closes the font, style, zoom, size, and color pickers and the overflow panel when they are opened by a click, and a reply in a review card shows its own author's color.
