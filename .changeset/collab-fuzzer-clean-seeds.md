Breaking collaboration upgrade. Collaboration rooms keep an accept or reject on all the text its reviewer saw while a peer types inside the change, keep deleted content controls and joined text deleted, keep blocks in place around content controls, and keep the caret beside a tab you just inserted; this is part of the format docx-collaboration:1.5.3.1 upgrade. Export saved rooms with the previous compatible release, then reseed fresh rooms. See https://docx-editor.dev/pro/collaboration-versions#upgrade-rooms-to-format-1531.
