A bold or italic face that stands in as a synthesized style of another face of its family again keeps the end-of-cell line height and East Asian punctuation fitting. A face that your font resolver substitutes for a family with its own faces now paints in that face. PDF export reports faces drawn from another face of their family as partial coverage, so strict font policy refuses them. An English family name such as `Meiryo` finds faces supplied under its localized name.
