The font size − and + buttons stay enabled for a selection with several sizes, and each press grows or shrinks every run from its own size. New `increaseFontSize` and `decreaseFontSize` commands and Ctrl+Shift+> and Ctrl+Shift+< shortcuts do the same, and a header or footer paragraph reads its size from its paragraph style.
