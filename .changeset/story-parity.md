Editing in a header, footer, footnote or endnote now behaves as it does in the body: lists paint their markers and their buttons toggle them, tables and content controls work, indent and formatting read the paragraph you are on, and a table there answers the table commands. A footnote holding a content control, a bookmark or a hyperlink is readable again instead of vanishing. `selectionRects` and `spansInSelection` take the story's paragraph order as a required argument.
