Editing in a header, footer, footnote or endnote now behaves as it does in the body: lists, tables, content controls, page setup, formatting and indent all act on the story the caret is in, and every story's paragraphs are addressable by anchor. A comment authored in one of those stories is now related from the main document, so Word can see it. Pointer chrome follows too: content control outlines and the hyperlink popover resolve in the story they are painted in, and table row and column handles are offered in the story you are editing.
