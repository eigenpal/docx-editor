Content controls now render and edit as typed document structures, including inline and block controls, dropdowns, combo boxes, dates, checkboxes, placeholders, and locked or data-bound fields.

React adds content-control inspection, removal, boundary visibility, and form-fill navigation. Control contents participate in layout, selection, formatting, search, save/reopen fidelity, and undo while preserving unsupported OOXML extensions.
