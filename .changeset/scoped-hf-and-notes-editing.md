Scoped header/footer editing and footnote/endnote editing on React. Enter a header or footer to edit it in place, create or remove furniture, link or unlink a section to the previous one, and insert PAGE, NUMPAGES, SECTIONPAGES, or Page X of Y fields. Footnotes and endnotes can be inserted, edited in scope, converted, and configured from the notes chrome. Vue chrome for both is deferred.
