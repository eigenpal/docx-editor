'@docx-editor.dev/telemetry': minor
---

Add `@docx-editor.dev/telemetry`, which sends anonymous editor diagnostics, typing performance, and collaboration room metrics, including people and memory per room, to OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Sentry, or your own exporter. Ready Grafana dashboards for these metrics are available on the [Dashboards](https://docx-editor.dev/docs/2.x/telemetry/dashboards) page.
