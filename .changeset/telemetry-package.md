'@docx-editor.dev/telemetry': minor
---

Add `@docx-editor.dev/telemetry`, which sends anonymous editor diagnostics, typing performance, and collaboration room metrics, including people and memory per room, to OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Sentry, or your own exporter. Ready Grafana dashboards and Prometheus alert rules for these metrics are available on the [Dashboards and alerts](https://docx-editor.dev/docs/2.x/telemetry/dashboards) page.
