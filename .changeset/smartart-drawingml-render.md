Render SmartArt diagrams, drawing canvases, legacy shape groups, all preset shapes, custom curves, gradient and pattern fills, shadows, glows, dashed and compound outlines, and all line-end types read-only in the editor and in PDF export, with pictures and text boxes in their own shapes and EMF and WMF pictures as PDF vectors. The new `drawingModule` from `@docx-editor.dev/pro/drawing` renders charts (including radar, bubble, stock, and of-pie charts with trendlines and error bars) and lays out SmartArt saved without its drawing, and PDF export includes it.
