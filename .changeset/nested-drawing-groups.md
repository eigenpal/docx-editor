Drawing groups that contain nested groups now display their shapes, pictures, and text box text instead of a placeholder, and turned or flipped nested groups display their shapes and text. Group text boxes display vertical text, header and footer group pictures display in the correct position, and change bars and PDF links follow turned text-box text, whose painted position the `storyTurns` field and `turnStoryBox` helper give.
