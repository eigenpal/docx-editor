Text in shapes of a drawing canvas or a group now lays out in the shape's text rectangle, inset by half the outline width, also when the shape or its group turns, flips, or scales, and in each vertical text mode. Character borders, effect extents, and the outlines of inline VML text boxes now take their room on the line, also in right-to-left text.
